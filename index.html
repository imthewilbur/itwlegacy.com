The buy box

What we look for

If it's established and cashflowing, we want to hear about it. We're not locked into one industry — we're locked into businesses that already work.

Service businesses

Trades, home services, B2B, and anything essential to how a community runs.

Health & wellness

Practices and clinics with recurring clients and a team that can carry the work forward.

Any cashflowing business

If the numbers are real and provable, the industry label matters less than the operation.

An owner ready to exit

Founders approaching retirement or simply ready for what's next, who care what happens after.

Recurring revenue

Repeat customers or contracted work that makes next year's cash flow easy to trust.

Under $5M in value

Sized for a clean, financeable transaction through SBA 7(a) or seller-finance.

A team that can carry on

Staff or management already in place to keep serving customers through the transition.

One clear operation

A single location or footprint we can understand deeply — not a portfolio to unwind.

Not a fit for us: food & beverage and most retail. If that's your business, we'll still point you in the right direction.
